About Us

A better view of the places that matter.

XAerova is developing drone docks and browser-based tools that make aerial observation more practical for the teams on the ground.

A drone dock gives an aircraft a home base: somewhere to launch, return, land and recharge. We connect that hardware with a simple remote operations experience.

From agriculture and site monitoring to infrastructure inspection and nature protection, our focus is helping people see more clearly and make better-informed decisions.

OUR MISSION

Make aerial visibility easier to access, simpler to manage and useful in everyday operations.

THE PRODUCT

SkyMind

Your drone. Its home base. One connected experience.

SkyMind brings together XAerova’s drone dock and remote operations platform. It is being developed to connect remote launch, live viewing and return-to-dock through a web browser, with AI-assisted landing and charging between missions.

01

The dock

A dedicated home for the aircraft, designed to house, align and recharge it between flights.

02

The browser

A connected interface for live viewing, site status and the remote flight workflow, accessible from a laptop.

03

The return

AI-assisted landing and a coordinated return-to-dock process, designed to prepare the drone for its next mission.
